快速上手

  1. 打开 Lumc：Lumc 常驻在屏幕顶部的菜单栏（图标为圆形录制按钮），不会出现在程序坞中。首次启动时会自动弹出面板。
  2. 选择录制模式：全屏、窗口、区域或摄像头，并按需打开摄像头与麦克风。
  3. 开始录制：点击「开始录制」或按快捷键，倒计时后开始；再次按快捷键即可停止。
  4. 截图：点击面板上的截图按钮或按快捷键，框选区域或点击窗口，可添加文字、方框和箭头标注。

系统要求：macOS 13 Ventura 或更高版本，支持 Apple 芯片与 Intel 芯片的 Mac。

权限设置

Lumc 首次使用某项功能时，macOS 会请求相应权限。如果之前选择了「不允许」，可以在「系统设置 › 隐私与安全性」中重新开启：

  • 屏幕录制：录制屏幕和截图所必需。
  • 摄像头 / 麦克风：仅在你打开摄像头或麦克风时需要。
  • 辅助功能、语音识别：仅直接下载版的提示词优化功能需要。

Lumc 的全部内容都只在你的 Mac 本机处理，详见隐私政策。

常见问题

在菜单栏里找不到 Lumc 的图标

在带刘海的 MacBook 上，菜单栏图标过多时，部分图标会被刘海挡住。请在「启动台」或「应用程序」文件夹中再次打开 Lumc，面板会自动弹出。你也可以按住 ⌘ 拖动菜单栏图标，调整它们的位置。

已经开启了屏幕录制权限，但 Lumc 仍提示没有权限

先点击菜单栏图标重新打开面板，Lumc 会重新检查权限。如果仍然提示，请点击面板底部的「退出」，再重新打开 Lumc —— macOS 的屏幕录制权限有时需要重启 App 才会生效。

录制的视频是黑屏，或者看不到其他 App 的窗口

这通常是屏幕录制权限未开启导致的。请前往「系统设置 › 隐私与安全性 › 屏幕录制」，确认 Lumc 的开关已打开，然后重启 Lumc。

录制的视频没有声音

Lumc 只录制麦克风的声音，不录制系统声音（如其他 App 播放的音频）。请确认录制前面板上的麦克风已打开，并且已在「系统设置 › 隐私与安全性 › 麦克风」中允许 Lumc 访问。

快捷键没有反应

快捷键可能已被其他 App 占用。请在「设置 › 快捷键」中换一个组合；如果组合已被占用，Lumc 会提示你。另外，截图进行中时，其他快捷键会暂停响应，按 Esc 退出截图即可恢复。

提示词优化无法使用（仅直接下载版）

请确认已在「系统设置 › 隐私与安全性」中授予 Lumc「辅助功能」和「语音识别」权限。默认的 Apple 内置大模型需要 macOS 26 或更高版本并开启 Apple Intelligence；如果你所在的地区暂不支持 Apple Intelligence，可以在「设置 › 提示词优化」中填写 API Key，改用 DeepSeek 等第三方服务。

如何开启或关闭开机自启？

在「设置 › 通用」中打开或关闭「登录时自动启动 Lumc」。

如何卸载 Lumc？

点击面板底部的「退出」，然后把「应用程序」文件夹中的 Lumc 拖到废纸篓。你录制和截取的文件不会被删除，可以按需自行清理。

Getting started

  1. Open Lumc: Lumc lives in the menu bar at the top of your screen (a round record icon) and does not appear in the Dock. The panel opens automatically the first time you launch it.
  2. Choose a mode: Full Screen, Window, Region, or Camera, and turn the camera and microphone on as needed.
  3. Record: click "Start Recording" or press the shortcut. Recording begins after a countdown; press the shortcut again to stop.
  4. Screenshot: click the screenshot button or press the shortcut, then drag to select an area or click a window. You can add text, boxes, and arrows.

Requirements: macOS 13 Ventura or later, on Apple silicon or Intel Macs.

Permissions

macOS asks for permission the first time Lumc needs it. If you previously chose "Don't Allow", you can turn it back on in System Settings › Privacy & Security:

  • Screen Recording: required for recording and screenshots.
  • Camera / Microphone: only needed when you turn them on.
  • Accessibility, Speech Recognition: only needed for the Prompt Optimizer in the direct-download edition.

Everything Lumc captures is processed on your Mac. See the Privacy Policy for details.

FAQ

I can't find Lumc's icon in the menu bar

On MacBooks with a notch, some menu bar icons can be hidden behind the notch when there are many of them. Open Lumc again from Launchpad or the Applications folder and the panel will appear. You can also hold ⌘ and drag menu bar icons to rearrange them.

I turned on Screen Recording, but Lumc still says it has no permission

Click the menu bar icon to reopen the panel so Lumc checks again. If the message remains, click "Quit" at the bottom of the panel and reopen Lumc — macOS sometimes requires an app restart before the Screen Recording permission takes effect.

My recording is black, or other apps' windows are missing

This usually means Screen Recording permission is off. Go to System Settings › Privacy & Security › Screen Recording, make sure Lumc is turned on, then restart Lumc.

My recording has no sound

Lumc records only your microphone, not system audio (such as sound played by other apps). Make sure the microphone is on in the panel before recording, and that Lumc is allowed in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Microphone.

A shortcut doesn't respond

Another app may already be using it. Choose a different combination in Settings › Shortcuts — Lumc will warn you if a combination is taken. Also, other shortcuts are paused while a screenshot is in progress; press Esc to exit the screenshot.

The Prompt Optimizer doesn't work (direct-download only)

Make sure Lumc has Accessibility and Speech Recognition permissions in System Settings › Privacy & Security. The default Apple on-device model requires macOS 26 or later with Apple Intelligence turned on. If Apple Intelligence isn't available in your region, enter an API key in Settings › Prompt Optimizer to use a third-party provider such as DeepSeek.

How do I turn launch at login on or off?

Toggle "Launch Lumc automatically at login" in Settings › General.

How do I uninstall Lumc?

Click "Quit" at the bottom of the panel, then drag Lumc from the Applications folder to the Trash. Your recordings and screenshots are not deleted; you can remove them yourself if you like.
