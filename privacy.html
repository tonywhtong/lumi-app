隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 28 日　·　适用于 Lumc for Mac 1.0 及以上版本

Lumc 是一款在你的 Mac 本机运行的录屏与截图工具。我们的原则很简单：你的内容只属于你，它们不会离开你的 Mac 发送给我们。

一句话总结
  • Lumc 没有账号系统，也没有开发者自有的服务器。
  • 不包含任何统计分析、广告、崩溃上报或追踪类 SDK。
  • 录屏、截图、摄像头和麦克风内容只在本机处理，并保存为你本机上的文件。
  • 我们不收集、不出售、不共享你的任何个人数据。

1. 适用范围与版本说明

Lumc 通过两种方式发布，隐私做法基本一致，差异在于第 5 节的「提示词优化」功能：

  • Mac App Store 版：运行在 macOS 沙盒中，不包含提示词优化功能，不进行任何网络连接。
  • 直接下载版（经 Apple 公证）：包含可选的提示词优化功能，仅在你主动使用该功能时才可能联网，详见第 5 节。

2. 我们不收集的信息

开发者不会通过 Lumc 收集以下任何信息：姓名、邮箱、电话等身份信息；设备标识符或广告标识符；位置信息；使用行为或统计数据；崩溃日志；你录制、截取或输入的任何内容。

3. Lumc 使用的系统权限及用途

以下权限均由 macOS 在首次使用时向你请求，你可以随时在「系统设置 › 隐私与安全性」中查看或撤销。

权限用途数据去向

4. 保存在你本机上的数据

  • 录屏与截图文件：直接下载版保存到「桌面」；Mac App Store 版保存到「影片 › Lumc」和「图片 › Lumc」文件夹。截图同时会复制到系统剪贴板。这些文件完全由你管理，Lumc 不会上传它们。
  • 偏好设置：如快捷键、录制清晰度、标注颜色、开机自启开关等，存放在 macOS 为 Lumc 提供的本地偏好设置中。
  • API Key（仅直接下载版、且仅在你填写时）：加密保存在 macOS「钥匙串」中，不以明文形式存储。

5. 提示词优化（仅直接下载版）

这是一项可选功能：你按住快捷键说话，Lumc 把语音转写为文字，再交给大语言模型润色，并填入你当前的输入框。只有你主动触发时才会运行。

5.1 语音识别

语音识别由 Apple 的系统语音识别服务完成。如果你的 Mac 支持设备端识别，Lumc 会强制要求仅在本机识别；如果不支持，你的语音可能会发送给 Apple 进行识别，此时适用 Apple 的隐私政策。Lumc 不保存你的录音。

5.2 文本优化

  • 默认（未填写 API Key）：使用 Apple 内置的设备端大模型（Apple Intelligence，需 macOS 26 或更高版本），文本在本机处理，不会联网。
  • 自定义服务（你填写了 API Key）：你转写出的文字会从你的 Mac 直接发送到你自己配置的服务商（例如 DeepSeek、OpenAI），并使用你自己的 API Key。该请求不经过开发者的任何服务器，开发者无法看到其内容。你发送给该服务商的数据受该服务商的隐私政策约束，请在使用前自行查阅。
  • 为保护你的 API Key 和内容，Lumc 要求自定义服务地址使用加密的 https:// 连接（仅本机地址如 localhost 例外）。
  • 在设置页点击「测试」时，Lumc 只会发送一条内容为 ping 的最小请求来验证连接，不包含你的任何内容。

5.3 辅助功能权限

Lumc 使用辅助功能权限定位你当前正在输入的文本框，并把优化结果填入其中。Lumc 不会向密码等安全输入框写入内容，也不会收集、保存或发送其他 App 中的内容。

6. 数据共享

我们不会出售、出租或与任何第三方共享你的个人数据，因为我们本来就不收集它们。唯一的例外是第 5.2 节中由你自己选择并配置的大模型服务商。

7. 儿童隐私

Lumc 并非面向 13 岁以下儿童设计，也不会有意收集任何儿童的个人信息。

8. 你的控制权与数据删除

  • 在「系统设置 › 隐私与安全性」中随时撤销 Lumc 的任何权限。
  • 录屏与截图文件可以像其他文件一样直接删除。
  • 在 Lumc 设置页清空 API Key，即会从钥匙串中删除它。
  • 卸载 Mac App Store 版时，macOS 会一并删除它的偏好设置。直接下载版可在「终端」执行 defaults delete com.lumc.app.mac 清除偏好设置。

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 28, 2026 · Applies to Lumc for Mac 1.0 and later

Lumc is a screen recording and screenshot tool that runs locally on your Mac. Our principle is simple: your content belongs to you, and it never leaves your Mac to reach us.

In short
  • Lumc has no accounts and no developer-operated servers.
  • It contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or tracking SDKs.
  • Screen, camera, and microphone content is processed on your Mac and saved as files on your Mac.
  • We do not collect, sell, or share any of your personal data.

1. Scope and editions

Lumc is distributed in two editions. Their privacy practices are the same, except for the Prompt Optimizer feature described in Section 5:

  • Mac App Store edition: runs in the macOS App Sandbox, does not include the Prompt Optimizer, and makes no network connections.
  • Direct-download edition (notarized by Apple): includes the optional Prompt Optimizer, which may connect to the internet only when you actively use it (see Section 5).

2. Information we do not collect

The developer does not collect any of the following through Lumc: identity information such as name, email, or phone number; device or advertising identifiers; location; usage statistics or analytics; crash logs; or anything you record, capture, or type.

3. System permissions Lumc uses

macOS asks for each permission the first time it is needed. You can review or revoke them at any time in System Settings › Privacy & Security.

4. Data stored on your Mac

  • Recordings and screenshots: saved to your Desktop (direct-download edition) or to the Movies › Lumc and Pictures › Lumc folders (Mac App Store edition). Screenshots are also copied to the clipboard. These files are fully under your control and are never uploaded.
  • Preferences: such as shortcuts, recording quality, annotation colors, and launch-at-login, stored in the local preferences macOS provides to Lumc.
  • API key (direct-download edition, only if you enter one): stored encrypted in the macOS Keychain, never in plain text.

5. Prompt Optimizer (direct-download edition only)

This optional feature lets you hold a shortcut and speak; Lumc transcribes your speech, asks a large language model to refine it, and inserts the result into your current text field. It runs only when you trigger it.

5.1 Speech recognition

Speech recognition is performed by Apple's system speech recognition service. If your Mac supports on-device recognition, Lumc requires recognition to happen on your Mac only. Otherwise, your speech may be sent to Apple for recognition, which is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. Lumc does not store your audio.

5.2 Text optimization

  • Default (no API key): uses Apple's built-in on-device model (Apple Intelligence, macOS 26 or later). Text is processed on your Mac without any network connection.
  • Custom provider (you entered an API key): your transcribed text is sent directly from your Mac to the provider you configured (for example DeepSeek or OpenAI), using your own API key. The request never passes through any developer server, and the developer cannot see it. Data you send to that provider is governed by that provider's privacy policy; please review it before use.
  • To protect your API key and content, Lumc requires custom provider addresses to use encrypted https:// connections (local addresses such as localhost are the only exception).
  • When you click "Test" in Settings, Lumc sends a single minimal request containing only the word ping to verify the connection. It contains none of your content.

5.3 Accessibility permission

Lumc uses the Accessibility permission to locate the text field you are typing in and insert the optimized result. Lumc never writes into secure fields such as password fields, and it does not collect, store, or transmit content from other apps.

6. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any third party, because we do not collect it in the first place. The only exception is the language-model provider in Section 5.2, which you choose and configure yourself.

7. Children's privacy

Lumc is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Your choices and data deletion

  • Revoke any of Lumc's permissions at any time in System Settings › Privacy & Security.
  • Delete recordings and screenshots like any other file.
  • Clearing the API key in Lumc's Settings removes it from the Keychain.
  • When you delete the Mac App Store edition, macOS removes its preferences as well. For the direct-download edition, run defaults delete com.lumc.app.mac in Terminal to clear preferences.
